AI Privacy

How student data is handled by AI features

Data used: Student names, class, subject scores, attendance summaries. No biometric or payment data is sent to AI providers.

Purpose: Generate performance insights, draft comments, flag at-risk students, and power the AI Tutor.

Retention: AI prompts and outputs are stored for 90 days for audit, then deleted.

Opt-out: Parents can withdraw consent from the Parent Portal. Teachers can disable AI comments per class.

Providers: Configured AI provider processes data under a data processing agreement. No data is used to train public models.

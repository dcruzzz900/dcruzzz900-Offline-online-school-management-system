# V105 – Modern UI Integrated

Full school-result-system with My School Hub design language.

## Dashboards (all roles)
- **Admin** – metrics, gender donut, result progress, enrolment trend, quick access, activities, announcements
- **Teacher** – assignments, classes, performance charts, top students, activities, score-entry links
- **Student** – published results, performance + subject charts, attendance, AI tutor, activities
- **Parent** – children cards, performance, attendance, activities
- **Registrar** – status counts, admit student, status change, history
- **Platform** – multi-school metrics, platform actions grid, audit feed

## Design system
`static/css/modern-dashboard.css` + `static/css/modern-ui.css` (loaded from base.html)

## Apply
Unzip → deploy as usual → hard-refresh browser.
